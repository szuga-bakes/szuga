THE SMALL PRINT

Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version: if you sign up to hear when we open, I keep your email address, use it to send you one email when ordering goes live, and then delete it. I never sell it or share it for marketing.

Who I am

szuga is a micro bakery run by me, Alice Janusz, a sole trader in London. I'm the person responsible for your data (the "controller", in data protection speak).

You can reach me any time at alice@szuga.co.uk.

What I collect

Just your email address, and only if you type it into the "Know when we open" form. This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Why I collect it

To send you one email letting you know ordering is open. That's it.

I rely on your consent to do this, which you give by signing up. You can take it back whenever you like (see below).

Where it's stored

Sign-ups are collected using Google Forms and kept in a private Google Sheet that only I can access. Google stores this data on my behalf and may hold it on servers outside the UK, with legal safeguards in place to protect it.

I don't share your email with anyone else.

How long I keep it

Until I've sent the launch email. I'll delete the list within one month after that. If you'd like to hear from szuga again after launch, I'll ask you separately.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what data I hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it, or take you off the list, at any time

Just email alice@szuga.co.uk. There's no charge, and I'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

When ordering opens I'll update this notice to cover orders and cake commissions. The date at the top will always show the latest version.
