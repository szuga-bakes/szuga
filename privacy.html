THE SMALL PRINT

Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The short version: if you join my mailing list, I keep your email address to send you occasional Szuga news. If you order from me, I keep the details I need to bake and get your order to you, and my payment provider Square keeps a record of the sale and a simple customer profile, so I can see your order history and reward you after five orders. I never sell your data, and I won't send you marketing emails unless you've asked for them.

Who I am

Szuga is a micro bakery run by me, Alice Janusz, a sole trader in London. I'm the person responsible for your data (the "controller", in data protection speak).

You can reach me any time at alice@szuga.co.uk.

What I collect

If you join the mailing list: your email address, when you type it into the "Know when we open" form.

If you order from me: the details you give me to place your order, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery or collection details, and any notes about your order.

I take payments through Square. When you pay by card, Square automatically creates a basic customer profile for me. This can include:

  • your name, as it appears on your card
  • your card type and its last four digits (I never see your full card number)
  • what you bought, when, and how much you paid

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. When you check out, you'll be using Square's checkout, which is run by Square and covered by its own privacy notice.

Why I collect it

Mailing list: to send you occasional emails about Szuga, like when orders open, new products and limited-edition bakes. I rely on your consent, which you give by signing up, and you can take it back whenever you like.

Orders: to take your payment, make and deliver your order, deal with refunds or problems, and keep the financial records the law requires. I do this because it's needed to carry out your order and to meet my legal obligations.

Stamp card rewards: I use your order history to keep track of how many orders you've placed, so that when you reach five I can email you your reward. This is part of what I offer when you order, so it's covered by the same reason as the order itself.

Customer profiles: a simple record of who's bought what helps me run the bakery. It lets me recognise returning customers, answer questions about past orders and see which bakes are popular. I rely on my legitimate interests as a small business for this. You can object at any time and I'll delete your profile. I'll still keep the record of the sale itself in my accounts.

Ordering from me doesn't sign you up to marketing emails.

Where it's stored

Mailing list sign-ups are collected using Google Forms and kept in a private Google Sheet that only I can access. Google stores this data on my behalf and may hold it on servers outside the UK, with legal safeguards in place to protect it.

Orders, payments and customer profiles are handled by Square (Squareup Europe Ltd and its parent company, Block, Inc.). Square stores this data on my behalf and may hold it outside the UK, with legal safeguards in place. Square also uses some payment information for its own purposes, like processing your payment and preventing fraud. For that use, Square is responsible for the data itself, and its privacy notice explains how.

I don't share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it (for example, HMRC).

How long I keep it

Mailing list: until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete it. If I stop sending emails altogether, I'll delete the list.

Sales records: six years, because HMRC requires me to keep financial records.

Customer profiles: I delete profiles of customers who haven't ordered from me for two years.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • tell you what data I hold about you, and give you a copy
  • correct it if it's wrong
  • delete it, or take you off the list, at any time
  • object to me keeping a customer profile about you
  • unsubscribe from emails at any time (every email will tell you how)

Just email alice@szuga.co.uk. There's no charge, and I'll reply within one month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your data, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If the way I use your data changes, for example when cake commissions open, I'll update this notice. The date at the top will always show the latest version.
