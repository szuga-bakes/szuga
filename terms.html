These terms apply to every order you place with me. Please read them before you order. Your legal rights as a consumer aren't affected by anything here.

Ordering

I bake in weekly batches. Orders for each batch open at 12:00 on Saturday, two weeks before the delivery date, and close at 23:59 on the following Friday.

When you place an order, I'll send you a confirmation email. That's when our agreement begins. Occasionally I may have to turn down an order, for example if a batch sells out. If that happens after you've paid, I'll refund you in full.

Prices and payment

Prices are shown on the website and at checkout. You pay in full when you order. Payments are taken securely by Square, and I never see your full card details.

Delivery

I deliver anywhere in the UK by Royal Mail Tracked 24. All orders are dispatched on Friday and should arrive on Saturday. Royal Mail aims for next-day delivery but can't guarantee it.

Delivery costs £4.80 per order, or £8.80 if your order weighs over 2kg. The cost is shown at checkout.

Please make sure your delivery address is correct. I can't be responsible for orders sent to an address you gave me by mistake.

If your order hasn't arrived by Tuesday, please email me. I'll chase it with Royal Mail, and if it's been lost, I'll send a replacement or give you a full refund.

Collection

You can collect any order for free instead. Choose collection at checkout. Collection is on Friday before 3pm, and I'll send you the details in your confirmation email.

If you can't make it, please let me know as soon as possible. Because my bakes are fresh, I can't hold uncollected orders, and I can't refund orders that aren't collected.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel for a full refund by emailing me by 23:59 on the Monday before your order is dispatched.

After that, I'll have started buying ingredients and baking, so I can't offer a refund. The usual 14-day right to cancel online orders doesn't apply to food that goes off quickly or to items made to your specification, like custom cakes.

Custom cakes and commissions

Custom orders need at least three weeks' notice. I'll confirm the details and price with you by email before I accept.

Commissions over £50 need a 50% deposit, paid at least 14 days before the delivery or collection date. I'll confirm when the rest is due.

If you cancel a commission at least 14 days before the delivery or collection date, I'll refund your deposit. After that, the deposit can't be refunded, because I'll have started planning and buying for your cake.

Allergens

Everything I make is vegan, but vegan doesn't mean allergen-free.

My kitchen handles gluten, nuts, sesame, soya and sulphites. Some of the ingredients I buy may also be made in factories that handle other allergens. This means I can't guarantee any product is free from any allergen.

Full ingredients and allergen information for every product is listed here, and it's included with every order. Each item is packaged individually and labelled with the specific allergens it contains. If you have a severe allergy, please check carefully before ordering, and email me if you have any questions.

About my bakes

Everything is made by hand, so your bakes may look a little different from the photos.

Storage and best-before information is included with every order. Please follow it, as I can't be responsible for bakes that have been stored incorrectly or eaten after their best-before date.

If something's wrong

If your order arrives damaged, is wrong, or isn't up to standard, please email me within 24 hours of receiving it, with a photo if you can. That helps me look into it while it's fresh, and I'll put it right with a replacement or a refund.

Whatever happens, you keep your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. Your order must be as described, of satisfactory quality and fit for purpose. For more information, you can contact Citizens Advice.

If I can't fulfil your order

Very occasionally, something out of my control might stop me baking, like illness or my oven breaking down. If that happens, I'll contact you as soon as I can and offer you a new date or a full refund.

Stamp card rewards

Every order comes with a stamp card. Each order counts as one stamp. When you reach five orders, I'll email you your reward.

Rewards can't be swapped for cash. I may change or end the scheme in the future, but I'll give you notice first, and you'll keep any reward you've already earned.
