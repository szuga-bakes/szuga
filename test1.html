Hello, my little guinea pigs

First off, thank you for agreeing to be a part of my test box crew. I am really hoping all the cookies turned up in one piece and on time.

Secondly, a note on the contents. You'll see that you have 2x Golden Hour cookies and no Lavender Sunset, as listed on the menu. Unfortunately, this was a test run and I knew I must have messed up the peach dough when I was scaling up the test recipe. It turned out tasty, but it wasn't right texturally and I didn't have the time to make another batch, so I apologise for that. If you'd like to try it, it should be available to order soon!

You have received my 24/7 Cookie Box. All six ahem, five flavours in one box. The idea behind this menu was a cookie for every moment of the day, from Midnight to Morning, Midday to Dusk. When I was still suffering the horrors of the 9-5 (or rather 10-6) office job routine, dreaming up these flavours kept me motivated throughout the day. I'd think "What sort of cookie would I want to eat right now?" and the results are lying in your hands (or maybe your stomach).

These cookies really did consume my life throughout August/September and were all very much a process of trial and error. Sometimes many errors. But once I have a vision, I can't stop until I bring it to life. From the flavour, to the texture, to the aesthetic. But it's also about a feeling. I've left some notes explaining the feeling I wanted each cookie to evoke, whether that be a moment or a memory or even a person. Food is for me all about sharing experiences and spreading joy via the tastebuds. Realising that was where happiness and satisfaction lay for me is what gave me the courage to start this endeavour, so of course I wanted to share the results with the people who have been there for many of the moments that inspired these cookies.

Sam & Luke: I don't think either of you know how much you have inspired me over the years. To be a better person, to work harder, to enjoy life to its fullest. I know life moves fast these days but I'm trying to slow it down and I hope we get to see each other more over the next decade (and long into the future!)

Adam: You are my favourite person. I could say more but it would get soppy and gross. You have a very special place in my heart, and on this menu ;)

Alice x

Midnight Blues SIGNATURE

A black-and-white cookie with a jammy blueberry swirl, topped with vegan white chocolate and marshmallows.

What's inside

  • Black-and-white cookie dough
  • Jammy blueberry swirl
  • Vegan white chocolate
  • Mini marshmallows

How to enjoy

Storage
Keep in the fridge for up to 1 week.
Freezing
Freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, or at room temperature for 1-2 hours.
Reheating
Try microwaving for 30 secs.

Alice's notes

The Midnight Blues: that feeling of inexplicable longing for a certain... something. You can't quite place your finger on what it is, but there's an unfulfilled yearning there. A need to pull back the curtains and stare at the moon and the stars, gaze out into the infinite universe and chew on the void. You feel the blank space, an emptiness that needs to be filled, but it's not a despondency. There's a hope, a dream, a possiblity. Something at the end of the ellipsis, you're just not sure what it is yet... Or maybe that's just me.

This is my favourite cookie. It's a unique mix of flavours and textures, from the jammy blueberries to the fudgy marshmallows. I had a vision of this cookie late one night while on the hunt for the perfect bedtime snack. I was also really craving blueberries. This cookie leans more towards cakey than chewy, so it stays deliciously moist for days. Most importantly, it fills the void.

Morning Roast

A crispy oat and apple cookie with salted almonds, dried apricots and a sticky espresso glaze made with homemade apple honey.

What's inside

  • Crispy oat and apple cookie dough
  • Salted almonds
  • Dried apricots
  • Sticky espresso-infused apple-honey glaze

How to enjoy

Storage
Keeps at room temperature for 1 week.
Freezing
Freeze for up to 3 months and revive in the oven, wrapped in foil and baked for 10-15 mins at 180. Unwrap and grill for the final couple of minutes to get that oaty crunch.
Reheating
Stick it in the oven at 180C for 5-10 mins.
ALICE RECOMMENDS…

Best enjoyed fresh, ideal breakfast with a mug of hot coffee.

Ingredients

TBD

Allergens: wheat, oats (gluten), almonds, soy

Alice's notes

Are you ever gripped by the urge to walk out into the wilderness? Go find a log cabin in the mountains and live amongst the pines? Wake up to birdsong and drink your coffee in the crisp quiet of the rustic dawn? No emails, no spreadsheets, no endless doomscrolling. Everything slows down and it's just you and this moment, suspended in misty morning fog. You have nowhere to be.

I wanted a morning cookie. Something that would be the perfect stand-in for a bowl of cereal. Crunchy and fruity and sticky, this hits all the right notes for me. At first, I tried putting coffee in the dough, but it quickly overwhelmed the other flavours, so I kept it as a light aftertaste with the espresso-honey glaze, letting the buttery oats take centre stage.

GBF SIGNATURE

a.k.a Great Big F*ck-off Cookie. A vegan brown butter cookie with vegan milk and dark chocolate chunks and a crunchy malted crumb.

What's inside

  • Vegan brown butter cookie dough
  • Vegan milk and dark chocolate chunks
  • Crunchy malted crumb

How to enjoy

Storage
Keeps at room temperature for a week, longer in the fridge.
Freezing
Freeze for up to 3 months and revive in the oven for that freshly-baked cookie experience.
Reheating
Slap it in the oven at 160C for 10-12 minutes, until crisp on the outside and gooey in the middle. If you lack the patience, 30-40 seconds in the microwave should do the trick.
ALICE RECOMMENDS…

Lovely at room temperature or warm out of the oven.

Ingredients

TBD

Allergens: wheat, spelt, barley (gluten), soy

Alice's notes

The feeling you get when you spot your favourite person across a crowded train station. Or hear the opening notes to your favourite song. Something so smooth and reassuring, like a hot knife sliding across butter. A reminder that life is so sweet (and filled with chocolate chips).

This is my midday cookie, perfect to nibble on during lunch break with a good book. It's deeply comforting, like a hug from my best friend ❤️ With so many bold flavours on the menu, I needed a classic in the mix. But it couldn't just be any chocolate chip cookie. I needed it to be rich, fudgy, indulgent. The barley gives that warm, malted flavour that goes perfectly with the nutty aroma of the browned butter. Crucially, it had to have a good crunch on the outer crumb without losing the soft, doughy centre. Then, of course, a whole lot of chocolate. Thus, the GBF was born.

Golden Hour

A vegan milk chocolate and pecan cookie, stuffed with gooey banana, swirled with banana-miso caramel and topped with caramelised pecans.

What's inside

  • Vegan milk chocolate and pecan cookie dough
  • Gooey banoffee jam
  • Banana-miso caramel swirl

How to enjoy

Storage
Keeps at room temperature for 4-5 days, longer in the fridge.
Freezing
Freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge overnight or 1-2 hours at room teperature.
Reheating
Heat in the microwave for 30 seconds to melt the gooey caramel - but watch out for the banoffee jam!
